# Flow redesign A: a field of small moments

## Grounding and candidates
- Current `journal()` renders a serif hero, dated vertical rows, and an aside calendar. `calendar()` always shows the current month and links each active day to only its first entry. `filter` is category-only. `allEntries()` sorts user and demo records by date then update time.
- Candidate 1, **constellation wall**: scattered cards around an illustrated center. Strong visual signature, but arbitrary coordinates, overlapping variable-length text, and DOM/visual-order mismatch make it brittle for 10,000 records and mobile.
- Candidate 2, **flow field**: a wide, row-major grid of restrained thought cards beneath a full-width date instrument. Cards vary by content, not random placement. Chronology is explicit inside each card. This wins on reading order, scale, keyboard use, and buildless scope while still feeling spatial.
- Candidate 3, **continuous river**: a winding SVG path with entries attached to bends. Clear metaphor, but it recreates a timeline and couples layout to each entry's height.
- Chosen synthesis: candidate 2 with candidate 3's subtle current line only as background decoration, never as layout or navigation. No fake counters, activity streaks, or hard-coded personal facts.

## Reader experience
- Cool white canvas (`#F7F9FF`), near-ink navy text (`#20283E`), cobalt (`#496BFF`) and blue-violet (`#8068DD`) accents. Hairline blue-gray borders, translucent cool surfaces, tiny blue glow on active controls. Use a contemporary sans family with Chinese fallback; avoid editorial serif and paper texture.
- Header remains compact. Opening is a 2–3 line personal introduction, `Flow` name, one line explaining “随手记下小想法、观察和突然想通的瞬间”, and a clear `写一条` action. No large illustration or tall hero.
- A full-width **date instrument** sits between intro and notes. It has month name, previous/next month controls, a `回到本月` control, weekday labels, and a seven-column month panel of small numbered day keys. Empty days are quiet; days with Flow notes have a small violet notch, selected day has filled cobalt capsule, today has a fine outline. This keeps familiar calendar semantics while the compact instrument shape and notches distinguish it from a sidebar widget.
- Under it, category chips and a concise visible-result label lead into the **flow field**. Desktop cards use two columns and a deliberate 3:2 width rhythm in each row, alternating which column is wider. DOM order stays newest-first row-major; no masonry, absolute positioning, or randomized order. Card body shows short text up to roughly 180 characters, title, date, category, and one or two tags; full content stays in the reader. Long user text cannot push other cards out of alignment because cards are content-sized rows, not fixed-height boxes.
- If a card is a true one-line thought it still has generous breathing room; multiline thoughts use normal prose line-height. Treat every card as a readable linked article, not a decorative tile. Use a visible `阅读全文` affordance where excerpt truncates.
- Long essays retain their separate route and calmer single-column index. A small `长一点的思考` cross-link after Flow cards can point there, not a right rail of previews.
- Motion is limited to a 160–220 ms hover lift/accent and a short fade/translate on route entry. No continuous floating animation or parallax. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes movement, and focus-visible gets an equally clear outline.
- At <=760 px, use one column and a compact intro. Calendar remains seven columns with minimum 44 px target height when space permits; at very narrow widths, shorten weekday labels, keep numbers legible, and let the month panel occupy its own full row. No sideways card scrolling. Keep sticky controls out of the way of the mobile viewport.

## State and scale
- Keep the persisted `Entry` exactly `{id,kind,title,date,category,tags,body,updatedAt}` and snapshot `{version:1,entries}` under `flow-journal-v1`. Existing `kind:'log'` is displayed as `Flow`; `kind:'essay'` stays long-form. No migration and no user-record rewrite.
- UI-only `flowState = {category:'全部', calendarMonth:'YYYY-MM', selectedDate:null, visibleCount:24}`. A selected date is either null or a valid date in `calendarMonth`. Do not persist this state.
- Derive `logs = allEntries().filter(kind==='log')`; `categoryMatches` applies the current category/tag rule; `visible = categoryMatches.filter(date only when selectedDate).slice(0,visibleCount)`. Compute calendar notches from **all** `categoryMatches` in `calendarMonth`, before date or page limit. This makes calendar and cards describe the same category while never hiding late records in activity marks.
- Category change retains the selected day so users can compare categories on one date; show an explicit `清除日期` action and useful zero-result text when the intersection is empty. A day click toggles selection. Month prev/next clears selected date and resets visibleCount. Selecting a date resets visibleCount. `回到本月` resets month and date; it does not alter category.
- A day with no notes remains a button so selecting it produces a coherent empty state. Its accessible name includes full date and count for the current category. Month buttons describe their destination. Date is parsed via UTC-noon or integer year/month/day arithmetic; do not use `new Date('YYYY-MM-DD')` timezone-sensitive display.
- Render 24 cards, then a `显示更多` button adds 24. Filtering happens before paging. Thousands of records still need only a bounded card DOM; sort once per render and avoid per-card scans. Search remains global and independent of Flow filters. On save or cross-tab storage changes, refresh derived views without losing a selected day unless the month changes by explicit navigation.
