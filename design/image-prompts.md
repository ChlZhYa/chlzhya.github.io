Wide whitespace, Chinese serif headline, sans UI, monospaced dates. Header with orbital mark, Flow 浮流, 学习流水, 感悟文章, 关于, search. No hero eyebrow. Two-line hero 在学习中生长， and 在记录中看见自己。 Supporting sentence 一些每天积累的知识，和偶尔停下来想的事情。 Right-side matte green sculptural looping ribbon, fine grain, pale circular backdrop, handwritten a little, every day. Thin rules, open layout, no card grid, no product statistics. Footer 保持好奇，缓慢生长。 and Flow ©2026. All website controls and copy will be live HTML. Responsive implementation must remain practical.
